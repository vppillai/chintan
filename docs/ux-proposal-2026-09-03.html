Chintan · UX proposal · 2026-09-03

Notes first. Record from anywhere.

Today the app opens on a record button and hides the notes behind a pull-up sheet. This proposal turns that inside out: you land on your notes, the record control is one thumb away on every screen, and a note shows its recordings as the sources it was written from.

← click a phone, or these, to move between the three screens

What changes, and why

Landing screen

Now: a record button with a pull-up sheet you have to discover.

Proposed: your notes, with Record fixed in the bottom bar. Eyes-off recording still starts from the home-screen shortcut and the lock screen, which never went through the sheet anyway.

Finding a note

Now: a flat list, most recent first, and a separate Search screen.

Proposed: date groups, two-line previews, tag chips and a search field on the list itself that filters instantly from the offline corpus. Search stops being a place.

Reading a note

Now: body, then a transcript panel and a scrubber, with recordings numbered “Recording 2”.

Proposed: the body is the document; each recording is a dated row with duration, how it was filed, a player and its transcript. The 4-seconds-times-4 progress polling goes away with the floating card.

Filing into a note

Now: you say it, or fix it afterwards via “needs a target”.

Proposed: “Record into this” on every note, and a chooser on the record screen — the backend endpoint for this already exists and has no UI.

Archive

Now: a separate screen reached from the bottom of the list.

Proposed: a chip on the library. Bulk select, archive and delete work the same in either view.

Navigation model

Now: home, sheet states, five routes and a back-guard that decides whether Back collapses the sheet.

Proposed: three tabs, one stack. Back always means “back”. The sheet reducer, the strip and the back guard are deleted.

Roughly a third of the frontend goes, and the part that stays is the part the tests prove.

Plan

  1. Shell: tab bar, three routes plus the note route; delete sheet, strip, back guard, Home and Search screens. One PR, behind nothing — the old screens go the same day.
  2. Library: grouped list, search field over the offline corpus, tag and archived chips, inline filing row; bulk select carries over as is.
  3. Note: recordings as dated rows with player and transcript; “Record into this”; autosave in-flight guard.
  4. Record: target chooser; stop returns to Notes.
  5. Cleanup: Background Sync, dead mutation kinds, unused API wrappers, the WebKit Playwright project for the non-mic specs.

Each step is a PR that passes the existing suite; the e2e specs for capture, notes and archive are rewritten as their screens move.
