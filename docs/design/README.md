# Design

How each part of the system is meant to work, one document per part, kept
current with the code: each names the files that implement it, and CI's
contract and conformance tests hold the wire they describe to the backend.
Read `README.md` at the repository root for the system as a whole,
`docs/api/openapi.yaml` for the wire, `docs/backlog.md` for what is planned
and `docs/history/` for the dated reports these answer.
`specs/` holds the dated design specs — current behaviour, the options
considered and the recommendation — each folder with its own index.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `api-conventions.md` | The rules on every route: the Problem+JSON envelope and the one error mapping (`httperr/`, `handler/errors.go` `fail`, `api/problem.ts`), fixed sentences, the correlation id, the headers a client sends (`Authorization`, `Idempotency-Key`), body caps and field bounds (`handler/body.go`), cursor paging, the gateway's throttles, CORS and the one allowed origin, `info.version`, the shared `components.responses`, and the three contract tests with how their fixtures are regenerated (`CHINTAN_UPDATE_FIXTURES=1`). |
| `append-vs-autosave.md` | A voice append and an editor save landing on the same note: the one compare-and-set loop (`service.RewriteNoteBody`) and the one re-indexer (`service.RefreshNoteIndex`, both in `service/capture_edit.go`) that the pipeline's `append.go` and the editor go through, the append stamp, the `PutIfMatch`/412 invariant `repository/s3.go` rests on, and the one rule for when the editor's version moves without its words (`useNoteEditor` `adopt`). |
| `ask.md` | Ask: a question answered over your notes — the ranking and the prompt in `internal/ask`, the worker's `pipeline/ask.go`, the endpoints in `openapi.yaml`. |
| `auth.md` | Who a request is from: sign-in through the Cognito hosted UI with PKCE (`features/auth/oauth.ts`, `pending.ts` and its ten-minute pending entry), the token set and its single-flight refresh (`api/tokens.ts`, `session.ts`), passkeys as a hand-off to the managed login (`passkeys.ts`, `PasskeyCard`, the nudge), the verifier (`internal/auth/{verifier,jwks,identity}.go`: the JWKS cache, the claims checked, the tenant id as the token's `sub`), the device key as the other principal, sign-out, and the test conventions. |
| `capture-ux.md` | The recording screen and its shortcuts: `/capture` and the recorder's state machine (`features/capture/machine.ts`, `store.ts`), hold to talk on the tab-bar disc (`holdGesture.ts`, `holdTiming.ts`, `useHoldToTalk.ts`), how a recording survives (`buffer.ts`, `wakeLock.ts`, `ResumePrompt.tsx`, `useResendOnReconnect.ts`), the filing banner and Home's receipts (`FilingBanner.tsx`, `FilingRow.tsx`, `filing/`), and the captures poll and its ladder (`api/queries/captures.ts`). |
| `checklists.md` | Checklist notes: the one line rule and four-level clamp shared by editor and worker (`checklist.ts`, `cleanup/items.go`, `checklist-lines.json`), how a recording becomes items and merges into the list (`pipeline/clean.go`, `append.go`), the tasks mode and `SplitOutput`'s guards (`cleanup/prompt.go`, `service/note_clean.go`), the Items tab's grip, sub-items, Done and the one Undo rule (`ChecklistEditor.tsx`), and Tidy up list (`useTidyList.ts`). |
| `data-model.md` | Where every byte lives: the one table's `pk`/`sk` shape for each item kind (`repository/dynamo.go`, `dynamo_captures.go`, `dynamo_devices.go`, `dynamo_misc.go`), the `data` blob and the promoted attributes, `version` and its compare-and-set, the sparse `gsi1` with its `INCLUDE` projection and the hydrate step (`hydrateUnprojectedCaptureFields`), `ttl` and its grace, the bucket's key layout (`keys/keys.go`) and tags, the `PutIfMatch` invariant, and how to read a backup. |
| `home.md` | Home: the library screen (`screens/NotesScreen.tsx`, `screens/library/`), the brand row (`Wordmark`), the Pinned group and its reorder, the day groups, the row's menu and swipe tray, Delete with Undo (`Toast.tsx` `TOAST_MS`), the chips, the drawn checkbox, and what works offline; Back is `navigation.md`. |
| `idempotency.md` | Idempotent replay: `Idempotency-Key` on every mutating request, the route wrapper (`handler/idempotency.go`), the fingerprint, the claim and its lease, which answers are recorded and for how long (`repository.IdemTTL`). |
| `inbox.md` | The inbox: device keys and external recorders — `POST /v1/inbox/{audio,text}`, the key scheme and its constant-time check (`service/devices.go`), the throttles and alarms in `infrastructure/template.yaml`, per-key usage, the capture's timing record and `chintanctl latency`. |
| `navigation.md` | Routes and the stack: `ROUTES` and `LEGACY_ROUTES` (`app/routes.ts`, `/?mode=ask`), the linear Back model with Home as entry 0 (`useTabNavigation.ts`, `PENDING_TTL_MS`, `useBackGuard.ts`), tab moves and the parked second half of one, focus on a route change (`useRouteFocus`), scroll restore (`useScrollRestore`) and the render-fault screen (`RouteError`). |
| `note-screen.md` | The note screen: the header, title, meta line and the three segments under one sticky strip (`features/notes/NoteDetailScreen.tsx`, `NoteTabs.tsx`), the swipe and what it yields to (`hooks/useHorizontalSwipe.ts`), the Details/Share drawer with its tag and alias editors (`NoteDrawer.tsx`, `components/TagEditor.tsx`, the 32-item caps) and the pull-to-refresh arming rule (`hooks/usePullToRefresh.ts`), the on-screen keyboard model and the banner mic (`hooks/useKeyboardInset.ts`, `components/BannerRecord.tsx`), Find's mirror, and the Recordings tab (`Recordings.tsx`, `SelectionBar`, `MoveSheet.tsx`, `zipRecordings.ts`, Transcribe again, the scrubber). |
| `offline.md` | What the device keeps and owes: the IndexedDB schema (`offline/db.ts`, `DB_VERSION`), the note cache and its two rules (`notesCache.ts`, `useNotesCache.ts`, the idle prefetch of the first page's bodies), the edit queue (`queue.ts` `MAX_ATTEMPTS`, the terminal statuses, one entry per note, 409 not retried), `useResendOnReconnect`, `OfflineBanner`, and what works offline on each screen. |
| `pins.md` | Pinned notes: `pinned_at` and `pin_rank` as promoted attributes (`model/types.go`, the note row in `repository/dynamo.go`, the order in `repository/dynamo_notes.go`), `model.MaxPinnedNotes`, the one-request drag (`service/notes_pins.go`), and what a re-pin does. |
| `pipeline-deadlines.md` | Pipeline deadlines: the per-stage `context.WithTimeout` on every provider call (`pipeline.Config`, the defaults in `pipeline/pipeline.go`), the 840 s client timeout as the outer bound (`provider/groq_stt.go`, `openai_cleanup.go`), the retry inside each client on a 429, 5xx or reset under that deadline (`provider/retry.go`, its bounds in `provider/bounds.go`, `ProviderRetried`) and how it composes with the routing and ask attempts, why a deadline is retryable (`status.go` `handleProviderError`), `ProviderTimedOut`, and the tests that hold the numbers (`pipeline/stage_deadline_test.go`, `provider/bounds_test.go`). |
| `push.md` | Web Push: the `PushSubscription` rows and their ten-per-tenant quota (`service/push.go`), the four routes under `/v1/push`, the worker's one send per finished capture (`pipeline/notify.go`, title only), the service worker's `push` and `notificationclick` handlers (`sw.ts`), the Notifications card on You, and the VAPID pair in SSM, which `scripts/setup.sh` and `bootstrap.sh` install by default through `scripts/vapid-keys.sh`. |
| `regenerate.md` | Regenerating a note from its recordings: the one rule for which recordings qualify (`service/note_regenerate.go`), the worker's `regenerate-note` task and the by-words replacement of a used checklist's items (`pipeline/regenerate.go`, `append.go`), `POST /v1/notes/{id}/regenerate`, `chintanctl regenerate` and the ⋮ item. |
| `prompt-safety.md` | Prompt injection: what a dictated, typed or stored instruction can and cannot make the worker do — the fence and the shared data rule (`llm/`), the sanitisers on titles, aliases and tags, and the checks after each reply that hold whatever the model did (`routing/spans.go`, `pipeline.decide`, `cleanup.SplitOutput`, `ask.Sources`); the replay fixtures that prove each (`pipeline/injection_replay_test.go`); and the gaps left open. |
| `prompts.md` | Prompts: every prompt the worker sends — routing (`routing/prompt.go`), cleanup (`cleanup/prompt.go`, `provider/openai_cleanup.go`), checklist items, the two whole-note views and Tidy up list, Ask — the shared rules in `llm/rules.go`, each prompt's text, reply, guards and metrics, Whisper's spelling prompt, the prompt sizes, and the live evaluation a prompt change is measured with (`provider/live_eval_test.go`, run by hand with the instance key) and the re-record of the replay set CI then runs without one. |
| `retention.md` | Retention and the purge sweep: the four audio tiers as S3 lifecycle rules keyed on upload tags (`model.RetentionTiers`, `upload.RetentionTagKey`, `template.yaml`), the 90-day note-version window, the thirty-day archive and the weekly sweep (`internal/purge`) with TTL as its backstop. |
| `routing.md` | Routing: what the pipeline does with the router's reply — the candidate window (`routing.MaxCandidates`), the call and its retry, deriving the content from the spans, `pipeline.decide` and the fourteen rescue and refusal rules with every bound in `routing/bounds.go`, the transcription gates (no speech, hint echo, the short-dictation tidy), the `routing decided` log line, and the record/replay harness (`LLM_RECORD`/`LLM_REPLAY`, test binaries only). |
| `search.md` | Search: what the index row carries and how it is folded (`service/search.go`, `search_text.go`, `model.MaxSearchTextBytes`), `GET /v1/search`'s scoring, excerpt and window cursor, local ranking on every keystroke (`features/search/localSearch.ts`), the merge of the two lists, Find's mirror on a note, and how Ask ranks differently (`ask.md`). |
| `shell.md` | The app shell: the grid (`components/AppShell.tsx`, `shell.css`), the one live region (`StatusRegion`, `announce()`), the one toast (`Toast`, `TOAST_MS`, the replacement rule), `OfflineBanner`, the tab bar and its slots, `RecordingIndicator`, the service worker and install (`sw.ts` precached shell, `UpdatePrompt`, `manifest.config.ts`), and the tokens and themes (`styles/tokens.css` as the only home of colours and sizes, Ink & Paper and Nocturne, reduced motion). |

| `worker.md` | The worker Lambda: how work arrives (the bucket's `ObjectCreated` notification, the API's async invoke in `pipeline/invoker.go`, the four EventBridge rules), the `Invocation` envelope and the task table with each schedule, `MaximumRetryAttempts` and `CaptureDLQ`, reserved concurrency and timeout, the capture path's stages and resume, exactly-once through `version` and `AppendClaimLease`, the guard and the `reconcile-stuck` reaper for a capture left in a stage, the smoke task, the spend cap's effect, and what it emits (`docs/ops/metrics.md`). |
